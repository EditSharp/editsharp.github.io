Remarks

What a clip shows or plays comes from the input nodes in its graph, so the kind of content is the kind of input, not the kind of clip; VideoClip and AudioClip are the only two. Edits that can reach neighbouring clips (moving, extending, stretching, splitting, deleting) go through the clip's Channel, which keeps clips from overlapping.

Rational

Remarks

The graph and its keyframes stay at 1x; Speed scales the clip's time wherever it's evaluated, so setting it back to 1 undoes a retime. StretchStart(Time) and StretchEnd(Time) set it to the exact ratio of content to timeline ticks. Raising it trims the clip if a source now ends inside it.
