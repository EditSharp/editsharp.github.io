Remarks

It covers the clip's speed, the shape of its graph and every value in it, found through EditableAttribute metadata plus every node's keyframed values. It leaves out where the clip sits on the timeline and its head in-point: a head trim moves the in-point and slides every keyframe by the same amount, so keyframe times are hashed against Anchor(Clip), where they stay put. A nested timeline hashes as its reference, not its contents. Thumbnail caches compare fingerprints to find out which clips changed.
