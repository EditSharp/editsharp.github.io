Remarks

Frames are Hop samples apart at SampleRate, channels mixed to one. Each frame holds the energy in BandCount log-spaced bands from a Window-point FFT, scaled so that a full-scale sine gives a total of 0.5 (its mean square), and the largest sample magnitude of the hop. Nodes describe their effect on these frames (DescribeSpectrum(in SpectralContext, ReadOnlySpan<SpectralFrame>, SpectralFrame, ref object?)), and ClipSpectrum folds a clip's graph over them for a waveform display that shows the processed audio.
