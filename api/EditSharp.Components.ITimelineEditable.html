Namespace
EditSharp.Components

EditSharp.dll

Timeline edits that work on one clip or on a linked group of clips.

public interface ITimelineEditable

Remarks

On a Clip, an edit affects only that clip, linked or not. On a LinkGroup, it's applied to every member together, limited by whichever member has the least room.

Split(Time)

Cuts in two at a timeline time.

void Split(Time at)

Parameters

at Time

Where to cut; for a single clip, strictly inside it.

Remarks

A clip's halves keep its name and link group. A group splits every member that spans the time; members from the time on form a new group, and a side left with one clip is unlinked.

Exceptions

ArgumentOutOfRangeException

at isn't strictly inside the clip.

InvalidOperationException

A clip isn't placed.
