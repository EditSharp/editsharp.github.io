Remarks

One ffmpeg process encodes the whole render. Frames are composited in order on the GPU and written straight into ffmpeg's stdin as raw RGBA; audio is rendered by the streaming audio engine at the same time and written into a named pipe ffmpeg reads as its second input. Neither ever touches disk before it's encoded. Sources are read ahead of the frame being composited, and each clip's decoder is closed once the clip has passed.

Remarks

A source that can't provide content doesn't stop the render: it's drawn as a labelled placeholder (or plays as silence), and the report says which sources failed, why, and where.

Exceptions

ArgumentException

The blueprint is invalid: an empty timeline, a non-positive size or frame rate, a negative GPU index, no output path, or a fade-to-colour transition above the bottom channel.

InvalidOperationException

ffmpeg failed, or streaming the audio failed.
