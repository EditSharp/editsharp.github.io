How a layer (a video channel, or MergeNode's B) combines with what's below it.

public enum ChannelBlendMode

Fields

Average = 29

The mean of the two.

Clear = 0

Nothing: the result is transparent.

Color = 27

The layer's hue and saturation, with the luminosity below.

Darkens what's below to reflect the layer.

Brightens what's below to reflect the layer.

The difference between the two.

What's below divided by the layer: brightens.

Only what's below; the layer is ignored.

What's below over the layer, only where the layer is.

DstIn = 6

What's below, only where the layer is.

DstOut = 8

What's below, only where the layer isn't.

Multiply or Screen, depending on the layer.

The layer's hue, with the saturation and luminosity below.

Lighten = 17

The lighter of the two.

The layer's luminosity, with the hue and saturation below.

Modulate = 13

The two multiplied, alpha included.

Multiply = 24

The two multiplied: darkens.

Negation = 30

One minus the distance of their sum from one: 1 - |1 - below - layer|.

Overlay = 15

Multiply or Screen, depending on what's below.

Plus = 12

The two added together.

Saturation = 26

The layer's saturation, with the hue and luminosity below.

Screen = 14

Lightens: the inverse of multiplying the inverses.

SoftLight = 21

A softer HardLight: darkens or lightens depending on the layer.

Src = 1

The layer replaces what's below.

SrcATop = 9

The layer over what's below, only where there's something below.

SrcIn = 5

The layer, only where there's something below.

SrcOut = 7

The layer, only where there's nothing below.

SrcOver = 3

The layer over what's below: ordinary drawing.

Subtract = 32

The layer taken away from what's below: darkens.

Xor = 11

Each only where the other isn't.

Remarks

The first 29 are Skia's own blend modes, alpha-aware; Average, Negation, Divide and Subtract are separable blends applied where both layers are opaque, each layer alone where the other isn't.
