Remarks

Video and audio channels are kept in two lists, each indexed from 0 at the bottom, so a linked video and audio clip can move up or down by the same number of channels. A timeline can be embedded in another through a TimelineVideoNode or TimelineAudioNode; an edit that would make a timeline contain itself is refused with InvalidOperationException.
