Remarks

A tick is 1/254,016,000,000 of a second, so every common frame rate (including 30000/1001) and every common sample rate is a whole number of ticks per frame or sample. A long holds about 420 days of ticks. Arithmetic is checked and throws OverflowException, as TimeSpan does.
